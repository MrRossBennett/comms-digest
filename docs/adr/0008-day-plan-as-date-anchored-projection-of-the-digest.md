# Day Plan as a date-anchored projection of the Digest

Comms Digest will make the **Day Plan** — a projection of the **Digest** narrowed to a single date — the primary surface for a **Household Owner**, while retaining the **Digest** as a demoted, secondary backlog. The same date-parameterised composition produces both vantage points: viewed in the app on the day it describes it is "Today's to-dos" and is recomputed live so it reflects the latest evidence and resolved **Responsibilities**; composed the evening before and delivered it is "For tomorrow…" and that evening's plan is persisted as a fixed record of what was sent. Composition selects unresolved **Responsibilities** due on or before the date (so overdue items carry forward rather than disappear) plus items dated on the day, with a short forward horizon for "coming up"; undated open items remain visible in the app but are kept out of the delivered text.

## Considered options

- **Just filter the Digest by date at render time.** Rejected: it leaves no home for a delivered, frozen snapshot ("what did the text say"), and conflates the standing backlog with the dated, prepare-for-the-day moment.
- **Fold the Digest into the Day Plan entirely.** Rejected: the Day Plan is action- and date-shaped, which leaves **Good to Know** and beyond-horizon items homeless. The Digest is retained precisely to carry what the Day Plan deliberately omits.

## Consequences

The Digest's enduring, non-overlapping job becomes **Good to Know** and items beyond the Day Plan's horizon. A future raw **School Communications** archive ("everything that came in", where newsletter separation will live) is a distinct third surface, not the demoted Digest.
